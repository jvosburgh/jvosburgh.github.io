# Narratives

Great products solve problems. Great product marketing helps customers understand why those problems matter and why change is necessary.

Throughout my career, I have developed narratives that connect customer challenges, market trends, and technical innovation into clear, compelling stories that drive adoption and business growth. Whether launching a new solution, repositioning a product, or creating customer-facing content, my role has been to translate complexity into messaging that resonates with decision-makers and inspires action.

The narratives featured here demonstrate my ability to:

- Identify customer pain points and unmet needs
- Synthesize technical and market insights
- Develop positioning and messaging frameworks
- Align stakeholders around a common story
- Activate narratives across digital experiences, sales enablement, and go-to-market programs

## Featured Narratives

### GoVAC® MAX System

**Narrative Type:** Product & Solution Narrative

The GoVAC® MAX System narrative was developed to transform a complex industrial cleaning solution into a compelling customer story centered on operational efficiency, safety, and performance. By shifting the conversation from product specifications to business outcomes, the narrative helped customers understand not only what the system does, but why it matters.

Through strategic messaging, customer-focused positioning, and solution storytelling, the content connected technical capabilities to real-world challenges faced by industrial operators, maintenance teams, and facility managers.

**Key Product Marketing Contributions**

- Solution positioning and differentiation
- Customer value articulation
- Technical-to-business message translation
- Website and digital content development
- Sales and customer communication support

**Portfolio Focus**

This example highlights my ability to build a product narrative that bridges engineering complexity and customer value, creating a story that supports awareness, consideration, and adoption across the buyer journey.
