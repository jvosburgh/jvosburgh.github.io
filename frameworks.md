# Technical GTM Frameworks


## Market Validation Loop
Problem definition → signal scoring → customer interviews → narrative refinement → iteration.
Hypothesis
   ↓
Signal Definition
   ↓
Discovery Loop (Extract → Normalize → Analyze → Adjust)
   ↓
Problem Validation (Serviceable vs Non‑Serviceable)
   ↓
Cross‑Dataset Validation
   ↓
Synthesis (TAM, segmentation, variance analysis)
   ↓
Decision (Advance, Refine, or Kill)
   ↓
New Hypothesis for Next Loop
