# Technical GTM Frameworks


## Market Validation Loop
The process begins with a clear hypothesis about the operational problem and the segment experiencing it. From there, validation signals are defined—frequency of events, technical applicability, economic impact, and workflow fit. Each cycle of the loop includes extracting and normalizing real operational data, identifying anomalies, and recalculating factors that influence market size and product fit.

Problem validation focuses on determining which events are truly serviceable by the product, filtering out edge cases or non‑viable scenarios. Cross‑validation with independent datasets ensures the market size is defensible and not dependent on a single source. The loop concludes with a synthesis of findings and a decision to advance, refine, or kill the hypothesis.

This approach ensures manufacturing products are validated through evidence, not assumptions, and provides a repeatable framework for assessing new markets, technologies, and operational workflows.
