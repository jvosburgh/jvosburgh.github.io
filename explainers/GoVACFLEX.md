# GoVAC® FLEX — Compact Natural Gas Capture & Transfer System

## Overview
The GoVAC® FLEX System is a compact, self‑contained natural gas capture and transfer unit designed for pipeline maintenance, NGV refueling, tube‑trailer filling, and general field operations. It provides a cleaner alternative to venting and flaring by safely capturing, recovering, and measuring natural gas across a wide range of pressure conditions.

> “Safely capture, recover, and measure natural gas across pipeline and field operations—so you keep more product and emit less.”

## What the System Does
GoVAC FLEX draws pipelines down from high pressure to near zero, recompresses the gas, and transfers it into adjacent lines or storage assets. Its modular design (trailer or skid) makes it ideal for residential, remote, or constrained job sites.

## Key Technical Capabilities
- **Flow Rate:** Up to **110 scfm**  
- **Pressure Range:** Draws pipelines down from **1500 psig to near 0 psig**  
- **High Discharge Pressure:** Transfers gas up to **3600 psig**  
- **Self‑Powered:** Uses **6–8%** of captured gas as fuel  
- **Hazardous Area Certified:** Class 1 Div 2 / ATEX Ex II 2 G  
- **Compact Footprint:** Trailer and skid configurations  
- **Low Noise:** <85 dBA at 10 ft  
- **Temperature Range:** Operates from **–20°F to 113°F**

> “Draws pipelines down from 1500 psig… transfers gas… up to 3600 psig… Average Flow Rate: Up to 110 scfm.”
