# GoVAC® MAX — Advanced Natural Gas Recompression System

## Overview
The GoVAC® MAX System is a high‑capacity, self‑contained natural gas recompression unit engineered to eliminate flaring and venting during pipeline evacuations. Its onboard compression engine powers itself using a small portion of the gas it captures, removing the need for external generators and simplifying field deployment.

> “The GoVAC® MAX System is an advanced, self-contained natural gas recompression solution engineered to eliminate flaring and venting during pipeline evacuations.”

## What the System Does
GoVAC MAX draws down pipelines, recompresses natural gas, and transfers it into adjacent lines or storage assets — all while maintaining low emissions and high operational efficiency. It is designed for large‑volume evacuations where speed, safety, and emissions reduction are critical.

## Key Technical Capabilities
- **High Flow Rate:** Up to **600 scfm** for rapid evacuation of large pipeline segments.  
- **Pressure Handling:** Suction pressures up to **1440 psig**; discharge pressures up to **1000 psig**.  
- **Self‑Powered Operation:** Consumes only **3–5%** of captured gas as fuel.  
- **Hazardous Area Certified:** Class 1 Div 2 / ATEX Ex II 2 G compliant.  
- **Low Noise:** <85 dBA at 10 ft.  
- **Temperature Range:** Operates from **–20°F to 113°F**.

> “Average Flow Rate: Up to 600 scfm… Fuel Consumption: ~3–5%… Hazardous Area Compliant: Class 1 Div 2 ATEX Ex II 2 G.”
