# AI Workflows for GTM

I design and implement AI-powered go-to-market systems that help organizations scale market intelligence, lead generation, sales enablement, and technical content development through practical, outcome-focused workflows.

## What I Look For in the First 30 Days of an AI Engagement

The first month of an AI engagement decides whether the rest of it works. Here is what I am actually looking for in those 30 days, before anything gets built.

### Is One Process Worth the Effort?

Not a department. One process with real money attached, where the data exists and a result would matter to someone specific. If we cannot name that process in week one, we are not ready.

### Access to Real Data, Not a Sanitized Sample

You learn nothing useful from a clean demo extract. The value and the problems both live in the actual records, with all their drift and duplication. Getting that access is often the real first milestone.

### An Owner Who Wants the Outcome

A named person on the business side who is accountable for the result and actually wants it to happen. Engagements with an owner move. Engagements where the owner is a committee stall.

### A Defensible Number by the End of the Month

Not a production system. A finding. Enough analysis on real data to say, "Here is the recoverable value, and here is the source line that proves it."

That number is what funds and de-risks everything after.
