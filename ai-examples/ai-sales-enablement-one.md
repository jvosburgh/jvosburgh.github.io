# AI-Connected Sales Enablement & Dynamic Pricing

**Company:** Onboard Dynamics  
**Role:** Director of Marketing  
**Project Lead:** Jason Vosburgh

---

## Overview

Accurate sales execution depends on accurate information. At Onboard Dynamics, product capabilities evolved as Engineering released updates, while equipment availability changed continuously as units entered and exited the service fleet.

To reduce manual coordination between departments and ensure sales teams always had current information, I connected Microsoft Copilot directly to live operational and product data sources. The result was a system that automatically surfaced current product information, inventory status, and unit-specific details, enabling smarter sales conversations and more precise pricing decisions.

---

## Challenge

The sales team relied on information that was constantly changing:

- Product specifications evolved as Engineering released updates
- Sales collateral required frequent revisions
- Inventory availability shifted daily
- Service fleet units cycled into resale inventory
- Each resale unit had unique operating histories, ages, and run-hour totals

Manually keeping sales materials and inventory information up to date consumed valuable time and created the risk of outdated information reaching customers.

The organization needed a way to ensure sales teams were always working from the latest available data.

---

## Tools Used

### Microsoft Copilot

Integrated with:

- SharePoint product documentation
- Engineering update records
- GoVAC unit status dashboard
- Inventory and resale unit tracking systems

---

## Approach

I connected Microsoft Copilot directly to our SharePoint environment, allowing it to reference the latest engineering documentation and product updates as they became available.

Rather than requiring someone to manually revise sales collateral whenever product capabilities changed, Copilot could generate materials using the most current source information.

I also connected Copilot to the GoVAC unit status dashboard, providing visibility into:

- Current inventory availability
- Service fleet units available for resale
- Equipment age
- Run-hour history
- Operational status
- Unit-specific characteristics

This gave sales representatives immediate access to current information without requiring manual coordination between Engineering, Operations, and Sales.

## Business Impact

The sales team gained two critical capabilities.

### 1. Real-Time Product Knowledge

Sales materials were automatically aligned with the latest product documentation and engineering updates.

Benefits included:

- Reduced manual collateral maintenance
- Consistent product messaging
- Faster adoption of product changes
- Improved confidence during customer discussions

### 2. Unit-Specific Selling

Sales representatives could evaluate available equipment based on:

- Unit age
- Operational history
- Run hours
- Availability status
- Service fleet transition timing

This allowed conversations to be tailored to customer budgets, deployment schedules, and operational requirements.

---

## Dynamic Pricing Enablement

One of the most valuable outcomes involved resale units transitioning out of the service fleet.

Historically, resale pricing often relied on broad assumptions or flat estimates.

With access to detailed unit-level information, the team could evaluate each asset individually and price equipment based on:

- Actual usage history
- Equipment age
- Run-hour totals
- Current condition
- Customer requirements

This created a more accurate and defensible pricing approach while improving transparency throughout the sales process.

## Why This Matters

Many organizations use AI primarily for content generation or productivity improvements.

This project demonstrates a different approach: connecting AI directly to operational systems so it can act as a bridge between departments and surface business-critical information in real time.

The value came not from generating more content, but from ensuring that sales teams always had access to accurate, current, and actionable data.

By connecting product documentation, inventory systems, and sales workflows, AI became part of the operational infrastructure rather than simply a writing assistant.
