## Situation

Onboard Dynamics operates with a single salesperson responsible for the full revenue cycle, including prospecting, outreach, nurturing, and closing.

Generic outreach was not a viable option. Buyers included ESG leaders, Operations Managers, engineers, and service partners, each with distinct priorities and business concerns. In addition, regulatory requirements varied by state and country, creating another layer of complexity that made one-size-fits-all messaging ineffective.

Without the ability to add sales headcount, the challenge was to create an outreach system that was highly relevant, scalable, and largely self-managing.

---

## Solution

I designed and implemented an AI-driven outreach system in Apollo based on layered personas rather than generic campaign messaging.

The system segmented prospects using multiple dimensions:

- Buyer role
- Target company
- Geographic location
- Regulatory environment

Each audience received messaging tailored to the concerns most relevant to their position and market conditions.

For example:

- ESG leaders received messaging focused on methane reduction and sustainability initiatives.
- Operations Managers received messaging centered on operational efficiency and field performance.
- Geographic variations accounted for state and country-specific regulatory requirements.

Apollo's AI agent automatically generated and delivered outreach using these persona frameworks.

To ensure qualified leads moved seamlessly into the sales process, I integrated Apollo directly with HubSpot. Prospects who met qualification criteria were automatically routed into the appropriate lifecycle stage, eliminating manual lead handoffs.
