# Automated Competitive Intelligence System

*Onboard Dynamics | Director of Marketing | Jason Vosburgh*

---

## Overview

| Category | Details |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Tools Used** | Inven.ai, Claude, OpenClaw, and Telegram, chained into a single automated workflow |
| **Constraint** | No dedicated competitive intelligence function or headcount; market includes both known competitors and unknown emerging entrants |
| **Action** | Built a daily automated prompt and briefing system delivering competitive updates directly to Telegram |
| **Business Result** | Continuous, low-effort visibility into competitive and market shifts, enabling faster updates to sales enablement assets |

---

## Situation

I had no dedicated resources to continuously monitor the natural gas compressor market for competitive movement. The risk was not limited to known competitors evolving their offerings. New and emerging competitors could enter the market without being detected until opportunities were already at risk.

---

## Solution

I built an automated competitive intelligence workflow by chaining together:

- **Inven.ai** for company and market data
- **Claude** for synthesis and analysis
- **OpenClaw** for workflow automation
- **Telegram** for daily delivery and notifications

Instead of manually researching competitors on a recurring basis, I designed a system that automatically surfaced relevant changes, including:

- New market entrants
- Positioning shifts
- Product announcements
- Competitive messaging changes
- Industry developments

The workflow generated a daily briefing and delivered it directly to Telegram, providing actionable insights without requiring ongoing manual effort.
