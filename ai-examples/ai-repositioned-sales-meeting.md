# AI Meeting Analysis → Repositioned Sales Messaging

**Company:** Onboard Dynamics  
**Role:** Director of Marketing  
**Project Lead:** Jason Vosburgh

---

## Overview

Sales teams often rely on intuition to determine which messages resonate with prospects. At Onboard Dynamics, we delivered presentations to natural gas utility organizations on a regular basis, but lacked a systematic way to understand which parts of our messaging were influencing buying conversations.

By using Microsoft Teams AI-generated meeting summaries across multiple prospect presentations, I transformed individual sales calls into a searchable dataset. Analyzing patterns across those conversations revealed a clear shift in what mattered most to buyers and led to a repositioning of our core sales narrative.

---

## Challenge

Our sales presentations followed a consistent format:

- Marketing and sales leadership presented the solution
- The Chief Engineer addressed technical questions
- Utility stakeholders evaluated the offering

Typical audiences included:

- Engineers
- Operations Managers
- ESG and sustainability leaders

At the time, our messaging focused heavily on product features and benefits.

While individual meetings often felt successful, there was no reliable way to determine:

- Which messages consistently resonated
- Which concerns appeared repeatedly
- What prospects compared our solution against
- Whether buyers viewed the product as a necessity or an optional add-on

Feedback existed primarily in memory and personal observations, making it difficult to identify broader trends across opportunities.

---

## Tools Used

### Microsoft Teams AI Recap

Utilized Microsoft Teams recording, transcription, and AI-generated meeting summaries to analyze prospect interactions at scale.

**Data Sources**

- Recorded sales presentations
- Meeting transcripts
- AI-generated meeting summaries
- Prospect Q&A sessions
- Follow-up discussion notes

---

## Approach

Rather than evaluating meetings individually, I reviewed AI-generated recaps across multiple prospect engagements to identify recurring themes.

The analysis focused on:

1. Questions asked repeatedly by prospects
2. Topics that generated the most discussion
3. Common objections and concerns
4. Competitive alternatives referenced
