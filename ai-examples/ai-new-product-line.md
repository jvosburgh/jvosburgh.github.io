# AI-Driven Customer Synthesis → New Product Line

**Company:** Onboard Dynamics  
**Role:** Director of Marketing  
**Project Lead:** Jason Vosburgh

---

## Overview

What happens when valuable customer insights are scattered across hundreds of conversations, support interactions, surveys, and CRM records?

At Onboard Dynamics, customer feedback existed across multiple systems, making it difficult to identify patterns that could guide strategic product decisions. By leveraging AI-powered synthesis within HubSpot, I transformed fragmented customer feedback into a structured, actionable signal that directly informed the development of an entirely new product line.

---

## Challenge

Customer insights were distributed across:

- Sales call transcripts
- Support tickets
- Customer survey responses
- CRM records
- Sales notes

While each source contained valuable information, no single source provided the complete picture. Product decisions often depended on anecdotal feedback rather than comprehensive analysis across the customer base.

The challenge was to discover whether recurring customer needs existed across all customer touchpoints and to identify opportunities the market was not fully addressing.

---

## Tools Used

### HubSpot AI

Leveraged HubSpot's native AI capabilities to analyze and synthesize large volumes of unstructured customer data.

**Data Sources**

- Sales call transcripts
- Customer support tickets
- Customer surveys
- CRM notes
- Sales team observations

---

## Approach

Rather than reviewing individual conversations one at a time, I used AI to identify patterns across all available customer feedback channels simultaneously.

The workflow focused on:

1. Aggregating customer interactions from multiple sources
2. Identifying recurring themes and pain points
3. Comparing signals across sales, support, and survey data
4. Validating whether themes appeared consistently across customer segments
5. Prioritizing insights with clear business implications

This approach transformed isolated anecdotes into data-backed product intelligence.

---

## Key Insight

A clear pattern emerged among distribution-side natural gas utility customers.

Customers consistently expressed interest in:

- Smaller equipment footprints
- Lower acquisition costs
- Solutions tailored to distribution utility operations
- Easier deployment for smaller-scale evacuation projects

The analysis revealed a recurring unmet need for a smaller, lower-cost pipeline evacuation compressor.

While the organization had previously discussed this possibility, AI-assisted synthesis validated the demand signal across multiple customer channels and customer accounts.

---

## Business Outcome

The insight directly influenced the development and launch of:

# GoVAC® LITE

A product specifically designed for the distribution side of natural gas utilities.

### Results

- Identified a market opportunity hidden within existing customer feedback
- Reduced reliance on anecdotal product decision-making
- Provided engineering teams with evidence-backed customer requirements
- Supported creation of a new product offering
- Helped expand access to a new customer segment

The resulting product strategy was grounded in real customer demand rather than assumptions.

Many organizations collect significant amounts of customer feedback but struggle to convert it into actionable intelligence.

This project demonstrates how AI can move beyond content generation and productivity improvements to support strategic business decisions.

By synthesizing large volumes of qualitative customer input, AI enabled:

- Faster discovery of customer needs

- More confident decision-making

Most importantly, the insight generated through AI analysis directly influenced a tangible business outcome: the launch of a new product line.
